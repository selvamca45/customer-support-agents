# BluePeak Store - Shipping Policy
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Delivery timelines
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days for metro cities, 5-7 business days for all other locations.
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days, available in metro cities only, for a fee of Rs. 149.
- Orders placed before 2 PM IST on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM are dispatched the next business day.
- Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays.
- We currently ship only within India.

## Shipping charges
- Standard delivery is free on orders of Rs. 499 or more.
- Orders below Rs. 499 have a standard delivery fee of Rs. 49.

## Tracking your order
- A tracking ID is sent by email and SMS within 24 hours of dispatch.
- Track your order at My Account > My Orders > Track Order.
- Order statuses: Ordered, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered.

## Late or delayed orders
- The estimated delivery date is shown in My Orders and in the order confirmation email.
- Courier delays of up to 2 business days can happen because of weather, festivals or high order volumes.
- If your order is more than 2 business days past the estimated delivery date, contact support with your order ID. We open a courier investigation within 24 hours and send you an update within 48 hours.
- If your order is more than 7 business days past the estimated delivery date, you can choose a full refund (including any shipping fee) or a free express re-shipment.
- If an Express order arrives late, the Rs. 149 express fee is refunded automatically.

## Order marked delivered but not received
- First check with neighbours, family members or building security, and wait 24 hours, because couriers sometimes mark orders delivered early.
- If you still have not received it, report it within 48 hours of the "Delivered" status through My Orders > Report a Problem.
- The investigation takes up to 5 business days. If the courier confirms the package was lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund.

## Changing the address or cancelling an order
- You can change the delivery address or cancel for free while the order status is Ordered or Packed.
- To change the address: go to My Account > My Orders, select the order and click Change Address. The new address is confirmed by email and SMS.
- To cancel: go to My Account > My Orders, select the order and click Cancel Order. The refund follows the Refund Policy timelines.
- Once the status is Shipped, the address cannot be changed. You can refuse the delivery or return the item after delivery under the Refund Policy.
