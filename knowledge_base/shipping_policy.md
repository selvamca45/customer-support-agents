# BluePeak Store - Shipping Policy
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Delivery timelines
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days for metro cities, 5-7 business days for all other locations.
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days, available in metro cities only, for a fee of Rs. 149.
- Orders placed before 2 PM IST on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM are dispatched the next business day.
- Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays.
- We currently ship only within India.

## Shipping charges
- Standard delivery is free on orders of Rs. 499 or more.
- Orders below Rs. 499 have a standard delivery fee of Rs. 49.

## Tracking your order
- A tracking ID is sent by email and SMS within 24 hours of dispatch.
- Track your order at My Account > My Orders > Track Order.
- Order statuses: Ordered, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered.

## Changing the address or cancelling an order
- You can change the delivery address or cancel for free while the order status is Ordered or Packed.
- Once the status is Shipped, the address cannot be changed. You can refuse the delivery or return the item after delivery under the Refund Policy.
