# BluePeak Store - Product FAQ
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Damaged or defective products on arrival
- If a product arrives broken, damaged or not working, report it within 48 hours of delivery at My Orders > Report a Problem.
- Upload photos or a short video showing the product and the outer packaging.
- After approval (within 1 business day), a free replacement is dispatched within 2 business days. The damaged item is picked up when the replacement is delivered.
- If the replacement is out of stock, you get a full refund to the original payment method.
- Reports made after 48 hours are handled under the warranty process.

## Warranty for appliances and electronics
- All home appliances and electronics come with a 1-year warranty from the delivery date.
- For BluePeak Home brand appliances (blenders, mixer grinders, kettles), defects reported within the first 30 days are replaced directly by BluePeak Store.
- After 30 days, warranty repairs are done through our authorised service centres. Book a visit at My Orders > Warranty Service.
- The warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, or misuse.

## BluePeak Home PowerBlend 750W blender
- The blender will not start unless the jar is locked correctly onto the base. Turn the jar clockwise until it clicks.
- If the motor stops during use, the overload protection has switched on. Unplug it and wait 15 minutes before using it again.
- A burning smell during the first 2-3 uses is normal. If it continues, stop using the blender and report it as defective.
- Do not run the blender empty or for more than 60 seconds at a time.

## Sizes for footwear and clothing
- Footwear uses UK/India sizes. Each product page has a size chart.
- If you are between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size.
- Wrong size? You can exchange it once for free within 30 days.

## Product authenticity
- All products sold by BluePeak Store are 100 percent genuine and sourced directly from brands or their authorised distributors.

## Out of stock products
- Click Notify Me on the product page to receive an email when it is back in stock.
- We cannot reserve or hold out-of-stock items.
