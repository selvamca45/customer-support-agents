# BluePeak Store - Billing and Payments
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Accepted payment methods
- UPI, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, and popular wallets.
- Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to Rs. 10,000, with a fee of Rs. 39.
- No-cost EMI is available on selected credit cards for orders above Rs. 3,000.

## Money deducted but order not placed
- If money was deducted but no order confirmation was received, the bank reverses the amount automatically within 5-7 business days.
- If it is not reversed after 7 business days, contact support with the transaction ID and your bank statement.

## Duplicate or multiple charges
- Sometimes a bank shows a temporary hold (authorisation) for a failed attempt. These holds are released by the bank within 7 business days.
- If you were charged more than once for the same order, our Payments team verifies the transactions with the payment gateway.
