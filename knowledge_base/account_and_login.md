# BluePeak Store - Account and Login Help
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Reset your password
1. On the login page, click Forgot Password.
2. Enter your registered email address or mobile number.
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to you. The OTP is valid for 10 minutes.
4. Create a new password with at least 8 characters, including one number and one special character.
5. Sign in with your new password. You are logged out from all other devices automatically.

## Not receiving the OTP
- Check your spam or promotions folder if you chose email.
- Make sure the mobile number is correct and has network coverage.
- Wait 60 seconds before tapping Resend OTP.
- You can request a maximum of 5 OTPs per hour. After that, wait 1 hour and try again.

## Account locked
- After 5 wrong password attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for your security.
- It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or you can unlock it immediately by resetting your password.

## Changing your email or mobile number
- Go to My Account > Profile > Edit.
- An OTP is sent to both the old and the new contact for verification.
- If you no longer have access to the old number or email, contact support with your order history for identity verification by our team.

## Suspicious activity or hacked account
- Reset your password immediately and choose My Account > Security > Log out of all devices.
- If you see orders you did not place, or your saved details were changed, contact support at once.
- Unauthorised account activity is handled by our Security team, which responds within 4 business hours.

## Deleting your account
- Request deletion at My Account > Settings > Delete Account.
- There is a 14-day cooling-off period, and you can cancel the request during that time.
- Pending orders and refunds must be completed before deletion.
