# BluePeak Store - Refund and Return Policy
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Return window
- Footwear and clothing: return within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, with original tags and the original shoe box or packaging.
- Home appliances and electronics: return within 10 days of delivery if unused and in original sealed packaging. Defective appliances are covered by the replacement process in the Product FAQ.
- Non-returnable items: innerwear, opened personal care products, and customised or engraved products.

## How to request a return
1. Go to My Account > My Orders and select the item.
2. Click Return and choose a reason. For damaged items, upload clear photos.
3. A free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.
4. Pack the item in its original packaging and hand it to the courier. Keep the pickup receipt.

## Quality check
- Returned items are inspected within 2 business days after they reach our warehouse.
- Once the item passes the quality check, the refund is approved and you receive an email confirmation.

## Refund timelines
After the refund is approved, the money reaches you in:
- UPI and wallets: 1-3 business days.
- Net banking: 3-5 business days.
- Credit and debit cards: 5-7 business days.
- Cash on Delivery orders: bank transfer to the account you provide, within 3-5 business days.
- BluePeak Store Credit: instantly, if you choose store credit instead.

## Exchanges
- Footwear and clothing can be exchanged for a different size once, free of charge, within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock.
- Request an exchange at My Orders > Exchange.

## Partial refunds and rejected returns
- If a returned item is used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts or tags, we may give a partial refund of up to 50 percent, or reject the return and send the item back.

## Refund not received
- If the refund has not arrived after the timeline above, contact support with your order ID.
- We share the bank reference number (RRN or ARN) within 1 business day so you can trace the money with your bank.
