# BluePeak Store - Contact and Escalation
Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Customer support hours
- Chat and email support: 9 AM to 9 PM IST, Monday to Saturday.
- Email: support@bluepeakstore.example
- First response to emails is within 24 hours.

## When a case goes to a human specialist
A support case is passed to a human specialist when:
- The customer reports fraud, unauthorised transactions, or a payment dispute.
- The customer mentions legal action or a consumer complaint.
- The issue is urgent and could not be solved with the standard policies.
- The request is not covered by any BluePeak Store policy.

## What happens after escalation
- An escalated case gets a reference number and is assigned to a human specialist.
- A specialist contacts the customer within 4 business hours by email or phone.
- Escalated payment cases are handled by the Payments team. Account security cases are handled by the Security team.
